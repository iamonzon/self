### Why

I've always been type of person who enjoys writing. I had over 1500 notes in obsidian, yet for some reason never wrote my developer journey in a public blog.


I always found small blog pages very amusing. They don't feel heavy to read and makes you want to read more.

Recently I bought two domains and an [old-server](https://www.lenovo.com/us/en/p/desktops/thinkcentre/m-series-tiny/thinkcentre-m710q/11tc1mt710q) for my home-lab, I had this blog in mind since then. 

I started this blog with [Hakyll](https://jaspervdj.be/hakyll/) for fun with the idea of getting better in Haskell.

My goal for this blog is document some TIL, math journeys and engineering/leadership ideas.
